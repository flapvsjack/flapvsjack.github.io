Privacy policy

Spooners Hub (flapvsjack.github.io) · last updated November 25, 2026

This page explains what the Spooners Hub and the stream raids keep about you, and why. It's a fan community project run by flapvsjack, not a company.

What we keep

  • Your sign-in. When you sign in with Discord, Twitch or Google, we receive your account ID, display name, username and email address from that service. We use them only to know which coins and cosmetics are yours.
  • Your game data. Coins, raid chests you've won, cosmetics you bought, their levels, what your Spooner wears and its colours.
  • Chat names you link. If you type a !link code in stream chat, we save that YouTube or Twitch chat name with your account.
  • Stream activity counts. During streams and raids we count chat messages, emotes and raid commands per chat name, to level up your cosmetics. We do not keep the text of your messages for this.
  • Ban appeals. If you appeal a Discord ban on the appeal page, we keep what you wrote, your Discord ID and name, and the moderators' decision, so we can answer you and avoid repeat appeals.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information, and there are no ads or trackers on the hub.
  • We never see your Discord, Twitch or Google password.
  • We don't post anything to your accounts.

Where it's stored

Game data and sign-ins are stored with Supabase (our database host). The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Sign-in goes through Discord, Twitch or Google, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Ask in the Discord server (message flapvsjack or a moderator) and we'll delete your account, coins, cosmetics and linked chat names. You can also remove the Spooners Hub's access from your Discord, Twitch or Google account settings at any time.

Kids

The hub is meant for people old enough to use Discord, Twitch or YouTube under their own rules.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
